# SkyCircuit product direction

> updated 2026-08-29 · v0.0.0

## Identity
SkyCircuit is an original mobile circuit puzzle built around one satisfying loop: rotate conduits, complete a route, watch ignition travel through the circuit, then launch rockets.

## Audience
Primary audience: casual mobile puzzle players who value short sessions, readable controls, visual payoff, and nostalgic rotate-and-connect mechanics without requiring knowledge of an older game or brand.

## Free core
- Classic mode.
- Classic Circuit skin.
- First-run tutorial and replayable help.
- Local score and high score.

## Plus direction
Plus is not wired to purchases in the current prototype. The product direction is no ads, premium skins, Zen Mode, Blitz Mode, and later shared challenge/event features. The current build exposes the skins and Zen/Blitz as playable previews so gameplay can be validated before a store entitlement system exists.

## Roadmap order
1. Validate ignition feel, tutorial clarity, and skin readability.
2. Validate Zen and Blitz retention separately from Classic.
3. Add Daily Challenge with deterministic seeded boards.
4. Add store entitlement only after Plus content has demonstrated value.
5. Consider weekly events, leaderboards, and seasonal themes after the core modes are stable.
